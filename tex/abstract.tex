\textbf{Roman~N.~Lee, Matthew~D.~Schwartz and Xiaoyuan Zhang published an analytic
result for the Compton total cross section at next-to-leading order
in 2021~\cite{qed-processes-lsz}. This work independently recalculates
and verifies that result.}
The calculation starts from the QED amplitudes and performs their
integrations explicitly. The result includes the one-loop virtual correction
to electron--photon scattering and the emission of a second final-state
photon, integrated over their full final-state phase spaces. The electron
mass is kept exactly. Electron--positron pair production is a separate
process and is excluded from this total.

Starting from the Feynman rules, we carry out the loop and phase-space
integrations analytically. A photon mass regulates the infrared
singularities, whose cancellation is shown after matching the virtual
and real contributions in a common convention. On-shell renormalization
fixes the mass, charge, and external-field normalizations. The final
cross section is a finite combination of logarithms and polylogarithms,
including generalized polylogarithms defined by iterated integrals of
specified kernels. No loop-parameter or phase-space integral remains
to be evaluated numerically.

We give the intermediate primitives and endpoint terms, simplify the
analytic result, and derive its low- and high-energy expansions with
explicit remainder orders. Published total-cross-section formulas enter
only in the subsequent analytic comparison. Independent numerical
integration provides a separate cross-check of the analytic result.
