\section{Limits of the independent virtual contribution}
This section and the following soft and real-radiation sections expand
the separate contributions to the same total cross section. None of
the separate contributions is the physical total. They are combined
with the finite regulator conversion in
\eqref{eq:comp-assembly-common-regulator}, so that the photon-mass
logarithm cancels before a limit of the total is interpreted.
Define
\begin{equation}
 X=\frac{s_{\mathrm{phys}}}{m^2}-1=\frac{2\omega}{m},\qquad
 L=\ln X,\qquad \ell_\lambda=\ln\frac{\lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}}{m}.
\end{equation}
The contribution considered here includes the complete virtual amplitude,
with the on-shell subtractions, but excludes real emission.  Its
normalization is
\begin{equation}
 \sigma_{\mathrm{virt},1}=\frac{\alpha_{\mathrm T}^3}{m^2}C_V(1+X,\lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}/m).
\end{equation}
Thus its photon-mass logarithm has not yet canceled.  All coefficients
below are obtained from the independently integrated amplitude.

\paragraph{A smaller exact basis.}
The 116 constant-letter coefficients of the independent virtual answer
can be transformed back to 23 words in $X$, with letters
$0,1,-1,-2,-4,r$.  Inverting the constant kernel-map matrix and
substituting the result verifies all 116 original coefficient equations
exactly.  The resulting prefactors have the form
\begin{equation}
 R_w(X)+\sqrt{\frac{X}{X+4}}\,S_w(X),
\end{equation}
where $R_w$ and $S_w$ are rational functions, also linear in
$\ell_\lambda$.  Only the change-of-basis matrix is used in this inversion.
The published cross-section coefficients are not inputs.

\subsection{Low energy}
The defining kernels have convergent expansions near zero:
\begin{align}
 \frac{1}{X-a}&=-\sum_{n=0}^\infty\frac{X^n}{a^{n+1}}
 \quad(a\ne0),\\
 \frac{1}{\sqrt{X(X+4)}}
 &=\frac{1}{2\sqrt X}\sum_{n=0}^\infty
   \binom{-\tfrac12}{n}\frac{X^n}{4^n}.
\end{align}
These expansions, together with $G(0;X)=\ln X$, recursively determine
all required words as powers and logarithms.  At each integration step,
for $p>-1$, one uses
\begin{equation}
 \int_0^X t^p\ln^k t\,\mathrm{d}t
 =X^{p+1}\sum_{j=0}^k
 \frac{(-1)^j k!}{(k-j)!(p+1)^{j+1}}\ln^{k-j}X.
\end{equation}
The all-zero words are fixed separately by
$G(0,\ldots,0;X)=\ln^nX/n!$.
Multiplication by the exact rational prefactors and expansion of the
remaining square root produce only rational coefficients and
$\ell_\lambda$.  All negative powers and all half-integer powers cancel
in the sum.  The result is
\begin{align}
 C_V={}&X^2\left[-\frac83\ln X+\frac89\ell_\lambda+\frac{22}{9}\right]
 \\
 &+X^3\left[\frac{16}{5}\ln X-\frac{28}{15}\ell_\lambda-\frac{16}{3}\right]
 +O\!\left(X^4|\ln X|\right).
 \label{eq:compton-independent-virtual-low}
\end{align}
This expands the leading small-photon-mass expression obtained at
fixed $X>0$, with $\ell_\lambda$ kept symbolic. The omitted
power corrections in the photon mass are not uniform as $X\to0$.
It is therefore not a threshold expansion of the exact amplitude at
fixed nonzero photon mass. The regulator logarithm cancels when
this expression is combined with the real contribution.
It supplies a direct check on the infrared coefficient:
\begin{equation}
 A(1+X)=\frac89X^2-\frac{28}{15}X^3+O(X^4).
\end{equation}

\subsection{High energy}
The leading rational prefactor of every word is $O(1/X)$, so only the
logarithmic polynomial and constant of each word at infinity are needed.
The rational-letter words reduce to classical polylogarithms and shuffle
products.  Their inversion formulas fix, for example,
\begin{align}
 G(-1,0;X)&=\tfrac12L^2-\tfrac{\pi^2}{6}+O(L/X),\\
 G(0,-1;X)&=\tfrac12L^2+\tfrac{\pi^2}{6}+O(L/X),\\
 G(1,0;X)&=\tfrac12L^2+\tfrac{\pi^2}{3}+O(L/X).
\end{align}
For the root word, its independently established dilogarithmic form gives
\begin{equation}
 G(r,-1;X)=\tfrac12L^2-\tfrac{\pi^2}{18}+O(L/X).
\end{equation}
Consequently the coefficient of the photon-mass logarithm is fixed
completely at this order:
\begin{equation}
 A(1+X)=\frac{4L^2-2L-2\pi^2/3-5}{X}
 +O\!\left(\frac{L^2}{X^2}\right).
 \label{eq:compton-independent-ir-high}
\end{equation}

The logarithmic terms of the finite virtual contribution have also been
obtained.  Its leading expansion can be written as
\begin{equation}
 C_V=\frac1X\left[
 -\frac23L^3+(4\ell_\lambda+1)L^2
 +\left(-2\ell_\lambda+\frac{5\pi^2}{3}\right)L
 -\left(\frac{2\pi^2}{3}+5\right)\ell_\lambda+B_V
 \right]+O\!\left(\frac{L^3}{X^2}\right).
\end{equation}
To evaluate $B_V$, define the finite endpoint constants
\begin{align}
 K_a&=\lim_{X\to\infty}\left[G(r,-1,-1;X)-L^3/6\right],\\
 K_b&=\lim_{X\to\infty}\left[G(r,-1,0;X)-L^3/6+\pi^2L/6\right],\\
 K_c&=\lim_{X\to\infty}\left[G(r,0,-1;X)-L^3/6-\pi^2L/6\right],\\
 K_d&=\lim_{X\to\infty}\left[G(r,-2,-1;X)-L^3/6+\pi^2L/12\right],\\
 K_e&=\lim_{X\to\infty}\left[G(-4,r,-1;X)-L^3/6+\pi^2L/18\right],\\
 K_f&=\lim_{X\to\infty}\left[G(0,r,-1;X)-L^3/6+\pi^2L/18\right].
\end{align}
The remaining finite constant is
\begin{equation}
 B_V=9K_a-18K_b-6K_c-8K_d-12K_e-9K_f
 -\frac{19}{2}-\frac{2\pi^2}{3}+19\zeta(3).
\end{equation}
We evaluate these six limits directly from the root primitives before
substituting them into $B_V$.

Substitution of these constants into $B_V$ cancels the Clausen terms
and gives
\begin{equation}
 B_V=- \frac{19}{2} - \frac{2 \pi^{2}}{3} - 2 \zeta\left(3\right).
\end{equation}
This determines the finite virtual constant without fitting numerical
values or using the published high-energy total.

\subsection{The Born term}
For comparison, direct expansion of the Klein--Nishina total gives
\begin{align}
 \sigma_0&=\frac{\pi\alpha_{\mathrm T}^2}{m^2}
 \left[\frac83-\frac83X+\frac{52}{15}X^2-\frac{133}{30}X^3+O(X^4)\right],\\
 \sigma_0&=\frac{\pi\alpha_{\mathrm T}^2}{m^2}
 \left[\frac{2\ln X+1}{X}
 +\frac{18-8\ln X}{X^2}
 +O\!\left(\frac{\ln X}{X^3}\right)\right].
\end{align}
These formulas use the same $X$ in both limits and keep the cross-section
dimension explicit.
